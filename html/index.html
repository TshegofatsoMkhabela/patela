Patela helps buyers, suppliers and logistics partners coordinate multi-hop orders, track custody without mandatory apps or wallets, and manage escrow-based payments from pickup to delivery.

🛡️
The Honest Trust Boundary (Section 1.1 & 11.1)

A cryptographic scan proves that the two designated parties confirmed a physical custody transfer. It is not an independent guarantee that goods are undamaged, nor does Patela operate trucks or prevent hijacking. Balances are shown in South African Rand (ZAR); authorized licensed partners handle banking, conversion and money movement behind the scenes.
